# Company Knowledge Base

## Work From Home Policy
Employees can work from home up to two days per week with manager approval. Employees must submit their work-from-home request at least one business day in advance.

## Leave Policy
Employees receive 18 paid leave days per calendar year. Leave requests lasting more than three consecutive working days require approval from the department head.

## Reimbursement Policy
Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business travel expenses. All reimbursement claims must include receipts and be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

## Employee Support
For payroll-related issues, employees should contact the HR department. For technical issues with company equipment, employees should contact the IT support team.
